Dotz: Metro Edition Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Dotz: Metro Edition ("the App"), provided by PBrzezinski, is a turn-based paper strategy game for Android. Package: com.ppaul1989.dotz. Contact: apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.

Your Gameplay and Local Data

Gameplay takes place on your device. The App does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server to collect or synchronize gameplay data. Match state and selections are held in the App while you play and may be lost when the App is closed.

Advertising and Consent

Dotz: Metro Edition is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear in non-intrusive banner slots. Google Mobile Ads and its partners may collect and process information such as your IP address, approximate device location derived from IP, device and advertising identifiers, and crash/diagnostic logs for advertising, analytics, and security purposes.

In regions where European privacy regulations (GDPR/ePrivacy) apply, the App utilizes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request consent where required. Where available, you can review your choices using the privacy control in the App.

For more information on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosures.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience and is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The App requires an active Internet permission solely to load ads via Google AdMob and check for European consent parameters via UMP. The App does not request access to sensitive device hardware such as your camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or external storage files.

Contact and Inquiries

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact: apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.
