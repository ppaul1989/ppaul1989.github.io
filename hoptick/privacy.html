HopTick privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

HopTick for Android (package com.ppaul1989.hoptick) is provided by Paweł Brzeziński. Privacy questions: apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.

Reminders and transit data

The stops, lines, scheduled departures, repeat days and walking times you save are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app uses them to schedule local notifications and display the countdown widget. No account or developer-operated app server is required. You can remove reminders in the app; clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes its local data.

HopTick downloads public Łódź transit timetable and real-time data over the internet. Providers of those services receive technical connection information such as your IP address. The app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap services; map tile requests reveal the area displayed on the map and technical connection information to the tile provider. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Location and permissions

You can choose to use your device's approximate or precise location to centre the map and find nearby stops. Android asks for location permission. You can deny or revoke it in Android settings and use the app without this feature. HopTick uses the location on the device to find nearby stops; map tile requests may reveal the area you view. Notifications and exact-alarm access are used for reminders and can be controlled in Android settings. Android may delay reminders.

Advertising and privacy choices

Advertising is disabled in the initial release configuration. If enabled in a future release, a Google AdMob banner appears in Settings, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether ads can be requested and displays any required consent message before the advertising SDK is initialized. When required, an advertising privacy options control will appear in Settings so you can change your choices.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and interactions with ads for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Their retention and processing are governed by their policies. See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. You can manage the advertising identifier in Android settings where available.

Contact and rights

If you contact the developer, your email address and message are used to respond to your request and handle related follow-up. Gmail is used for correspondence. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data handled by the developer, object where applicable, or complain to the appropriate data protection authority. The developer cannot remotely access or delete reminders stored only on your device.

This website and changes

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information such as IP addresses to provide and secure hosting; see GitHub's privacy statement. This policy will be updated as the app's data practices change.
