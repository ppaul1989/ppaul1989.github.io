fiszkomania

Your words. One card at a time.

A local English practice notebook for adults guiding school-age learners. Add your own words, sentences or grammar prompts. Ask, reveal the answer, and mark the response.

Practise with a mix of new and earlier cards. Choose a time, correctness or amount challenge. Separate profiles keep their own words, progress and personal bests.

Words, optional profile photos and learning progress stay on your phone. Export a backup to a location you choose. An AdMob banner appears only below progress statistics; the privacy policy explains its data processing.
