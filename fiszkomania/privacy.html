Fiszkomania privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Fiszkomania, provided by PBrzezinski, is a word notebook for adults guiding English practice with school-age learners. Package: com.ppaul1989.fiszkomania. Contact: apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.

Your words, photos and learning progress

Your word pairs, grammar and sentence cards, local profile nicknames, optional profile photos, answer counts, mistakes, review schedules, challenge scores, learning-day streaks, planned break dates, voice-setup measurements and reminder preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Local profiles have separate word lists, progress and scores. Selected words can be copied between profiles with fresh progress. Fiszkomania does not send these records or profile photos to the developer or an app server. No online account is required.

If you choose a profile photo, Android's file picker gives the app access to the image you select. The app creates a small cropped JPEG thumbnail and stores that copy with the local profile. It does not request access to your entire photo library or upload the selected image. You can replace or remove the thumbnail in the profile editor.

You can edit or delete individual words and remove profile photos. Local records remain on your device until you change or delete them. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local notebook, profile photos and progress. You can export a backup to a location you choose and restore it later; the backup includes profile nicknames and photos. Exported copies remain in that location until you delete them. If you choose a cloud document provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Translation and speech

When you add a card, the app can suggest a Polish or English translation using Google's ML Kit on-device translation. The language model may be downloaded from Google on first use and updated later. Google says the text and translated result are processed on your device and are not sent to Google servers by ML Kit. ML Kit may send performance and usage metrics to Google. You can edit the suggestion before saving. See ML Kit terms and privacy.

The read-aloud controls use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. Whether a voice requires a network connection depends on that engine and your phone settings.

Optional voice answers and dictation use the speech-recognition service installed on your Android phone. Depending on that service and its settings, audio may be processed on your device or by the service provider's servers. Fiszkomania does not save audio recordings or operate a speech server. Microphone access is requested when you enable voice answers or dictation, and listening stops when you leave practice or the app. Practice may send vocabulary hints to the recognition provider to improve transcription. Unrecognised speech can be retried or checked manually without automatically marking an answer wrong. Voice recognition is not a pronunciation assessment. Topic suggestions are generated locally from vocabulary and do not send your cards to an AI service.

Optional profile voice setup measures background noise, voice volume and pauses locally. Setup audio is processed in memory and discarded; it is not sent to a speech provider. Only the resulting measurements and microphone settings are saved with the profile and included in exported notebook backups. On compatible Android devices and speech services, practice can apply volume adjustment and optional device noise reduction before passing audio to the speech service described above. You can repeat or reset voice setup in the app.

Recognized text used for a practice answer is temporary. Text dictated into the card editor becomes part of your local notebook only if you save the card, and is then handled like other card text, including in any backup you export. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings and continue using the app.

Learning reminders

Optional streak reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. The app asks for Android notification permission when you enable reminders. Reminders pause during approved breaks and after the relevant profile practises that day. Notification text does not include profile names or vocabulary. You can change the time or turn reminders off in the app, or revoke notification permission in Android settings.

Advertising

The main hub's sponsored tile and the area below Progress statistics may contain AdMob banners. Practice and word entry do not contain ads. Google Mobile Ads and its consent tools may process information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, diagnostic information and ad interactions. This processing is separate from your learning notebook.

The app currently requests child-directed, under-age-of-consent advertising treatment and a general-audience content rating as a conservative advertising configuration. These settings restrict advertising treatment; they do not mean that no advertising data is processed. Where consent or privacy controls apply, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform. A privacy choices control appears in Settings when Google requires it.

Advertising SDK data may be collected and shared with Google and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app does not operate a separate analytics or user-account server.

See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Google's processing and retention are governed by its policies and the applicable advertising settings. You can manage or delete the advertising identifier in Android's privacy settings where available. This does not delete records Google or its partners may already hold. Use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for requests concerning advertising data; the developer does not have access to individual advertising records.

Children and permissions

The app is intended to be operated by an adult during practice. Profiles can use nicknames and do not require real names. The app does not ask for age, contact details or location permission. Microphone permission is optional and used only for the voice input described above. Profile names and competition scores stay on the phone unless you export a backup. Its word notebook can be used offline; advertising uses an internet connection.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Send privacy questions to apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.
