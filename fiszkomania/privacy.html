Fiszkomania privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Fiszkomania, provided by PBrzezinski, is a word notebook for adults guiding English practice with school-age learners. Package: com.ppaul1989.fiszkomania. Contact: apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.

Your words, photos and learning progress

Your word pairs, grammar and sentence cards, local profile nicknames, optional profile photos, answer counts, mistakes, review schedules and challenge scores are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Local profiles have separate word lists, progress and scores. Selected words can be copied between profiles with fresh progress. Fiszkomania does not send these records or profile photos to the developer or an app server. No online account is required.

If you choose a profile photo, Android's file picker gives the app access to the image you select. The app creates a small cropped JPEG thumbnail and stores that copy with the local profile. It does not request access to your entire photo library or upload the selected image. You can replace or remove the thumbnail in the profile editor.

You can edit or delete individual words and remove profile photos. Local records remain on your device until you change or delete them. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local notebook, profile photos and progress. You can export a backup to a location you choose and restore it later; the backup includes profile nicknames and photos. Exported copies remain in that location until you delete them. If you choose a cloud document provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Translation and speech

When you add a card, the app can suggest a Polish or English translation using Google's ML Kit on-device translation. The language model may be downloaded from Google on first use and updated later. Google says the text and translated result are processed on your device and are not sent to Google servers by ML Kit. ML Kit may send performance and usage metrics to Google. You can edit the suggestion before saving. See ML Kit terms and privacy.

The read-aloud controls use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. Whether a voice requires a network connection depends on that engine and your phone settings. The app does not record your voice or request microphone access.

Advertising

The progress screen contains an AdMob banner. Practice and word entry do not contain ads. Google Mobile Ads and its consent tools may process information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, diagnostic information and ad interactions. This processing is separate from your learning notebook.

The app currently requests child-directed, under-age-of-consent advertising treatment and a general-audience content rating as a conservative advertising configuration. These settings restrict advertising treatment; they do not mean that no advertising data is processed. Where consent or privacy controls apply, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform. A privacy choices control appears on the progress screen when Google requires it.

Advertising SDK data may be collected and shared with Google and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app does not operate a separate analytics or user-account server.

See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Google's processing and retention are governed by its policies and the applicable advertising settings. You can manage or delete the advertising identifier in Android's privacy settings where available. This does not delete records Google or its partners may already hold. Use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for requests concerning advertising data; the developer does not have access to individual advertising records.

Children and permissions

The app is intended to be operated by an adult during practice. Profiles can use nicknames and do not require real names. The app does not ask for age, contact details, microphone recordings or location permission. Profile names and competition scores stay on the phone unless you export a backup. Its word notebook can be used offline; advertising uses an internet connection.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Send privacy questions to apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.
